Three steps, about a minute

  1. Click "Explore the example case" below. A finished family case opens: 14 sheets, 3 files, and the amount that may come off the bills.
  2. Look at the findings. Each one shows the sheet and lines with the error, the law or hospital policy section behind it when there is one, and which Nemotron model found and read the sheet. "What these lines mean" explains the lines in plain words.
  3. Then open "Letter: request itemized bill" and "Call cheat sheet": a ready letter to the hospital and notes for the phone call.
