Section 2. What this plan covers

2.1 Hospital care
Medically necessary inpatient care for childbirth and delivery is covered.
Care for a newborn child during the mother's covered inpatient stay is covered.
Covered services are paid at the in-network level after the deductible, minus coinsurance.

2.2 Prior authorization
Some planned hospital stays need prior authorization from the plan.
Prior authorization is not required for a hospital stay for childbirth of up to 48 hours after a vaginal delivery or 96 hours after a cesarean section.
In emergency situations or when prior authorization cannot be obtained, covered services will not be denied solely for lack of prior authorization.

Section 7. If we deny a claim: appeals

7.1 How to appeal
You may file an appeal within 180 days after you receive notice of a denial.
Send your appeal in writing to: Appeals Department, Sample Health Plan, PO Box 12345, Riverton, ST 12345.
Include your name, member ID, claim number, and the reason you disagree. You may include documents, records and written comments.
You may ask for free copies of all documents, records and other information relevant to your claim.

7.2 When we decide
We will decide an appeal about care you already received within 60 days after we receive it.
If we uphold the denial, our letter will explain your right to an external review by an independent reviewer.
